# Code of conduct

The Mass AI Atlas is a map of a real community, built by people from that community. The
standard we hold ourselves to is the one we would want from any organization listed in it.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make taking part in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and
healthy community.

## Our standards

Behavior that helps this community:

- Showing empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility, apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from
  the experience.
- Focusing on what serves the community as a whole, not only on what serves us as
  individuals.
- Disclosing an affiliation when suggesting or defending an entry. Suggesting an
  organization you are part of is welcome; concealing that you are part of it is not.

Behavior that is not acceptable:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing other people's private information, such as a physical or email address,
  without their explicit permission.
- Sustained pressure to list, delist, or re-describe an entry for commercial advantage,
  including on behalf of a client or employer.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

## Scope

This code applies in every space this project uses: issues, pull requests, commit messages,
code review, and the dataset itself. It also applies when someone is representing the
project in public, such as posting from an official account or speaking about the atlas at
an event.

It does **not** govern the organizations listed in the atlas. An entry is a factual record
of a real organization, not an endorsement of it, and a dispute about an organization's own
conduct belongs with that organization.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards, and will take
fair corrective action in response to behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening,
offensive, or harmful. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, code, issues, and
other contributions that do not align with this code, and will explain their reasons when
doing so.

### Reporting

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior to
**conduct@the-open-accelerator.com**.

> **Running a copy of this template?** That address reaches The Open Accelerator, not you.
> Replace it before you invite anyone to contribute — an unanswered report is worse than no
> policy at all.

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers are obliged
to respect the privacy and security of anyone who reports an incident. If a report concerns
a maintainer, it will be handled by someone else.

Do not use a public issue to report a conduct concern. A public issue exposes the
reporter and forecloses a private resolution.

### What happens next

Maintainers will follow these guidelines in deciding the consequences of any breach:

| | Impact | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Correction** | Language or behavior judged unprofessional or unwelcome. | A private, written warning explaining why the behavior was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested. |
| **2. Warning** | A single incident, or a repeated pattern. | A warning, with consequences for continued behavior. No interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited contact with those enforcing this code, for a stated period. Breaking these terms may lead to a ban. |
| **3. Temporary ban** | A serious breach, including sustained inappropriate behavior. | A temporary ban from any interaction with the community for a stated period. Breaking these terms may lead to a permanent ban. |
| **4. Permanent ban** | A pattern of breaches, sustained harassment, or aggression toward an individual or a group. | A permanent ban from any public interaction with the community. |
